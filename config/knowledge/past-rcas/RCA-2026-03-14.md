# Withdrawals declined after a network change slowed host authorization
- Id: RCA-2026-03-14
- Date: 2026-03-14
- Component: withdrawal-p1
- Exception: HostAuthTimeoutException
- Root cause: A firewall change added about 300 ms to every call to the authorization gateway, which pushed its response time above the withdrawal service's host authorization timeout.
- Resolution: The firewall rule was rolled back and the timeout was raised to leave headroom above the gateway's slowest normal response.

## What happened
Customers at many machines had withdrawals declined for forty minutes. Balance inquiries worked. The withdrawal service logged host authorization timeouts on every attempt.
