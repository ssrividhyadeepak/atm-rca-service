# Deposits were not credited because ledger maintenance overran
- Id: RCA-2026-06-18
- Date: 2026-06-18
- Component: deposit-p1
- Exception: LedgerPostingException
- Root cause: The ledger's nightly maintenance ran ninety minutes past its window because of a long index rebuild, so credit postings were refused with status 503 for longer than usual.
- Resolution: The reconciliation job posted all pending credits once the ledger was back. The ledger team now alerts when maintenance overruns.

## What happened
Deposit postings failed from the start of the maintenance window until well after it should have ended.
