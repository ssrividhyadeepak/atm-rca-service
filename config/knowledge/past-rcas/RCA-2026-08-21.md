# Balance inquiries timed out during a core banking batch run
- Id: RCA-2026-08-21
- Date: 2026-08-21
- Component: balance-p1
- Exception: CoreBankingTimeoutException
- Root cause: An unscheduled statement batch on core banking held locks on the balance tables, so balance calls took longer than the two second timeout.
- Resolution: The batch was moved to the overnight window. Batches outside that window now need approval.

## What happened
For about an hour balance inquiries timed out or were served from the fallback.
